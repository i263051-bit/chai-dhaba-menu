OUR STORY & TRADITION

SERVING WARMTH, TRADITION, AND COMMUNITY SINCE 2010.

HOW IT ALL STARTED

Founded in 2010, Chai Corner Dhaba started as a small roadside stall with just a single stove, a big brass kettle, and a passion for authentic tea. Over the years, it has grown into a favorite hangout spot for students, families, and tea lovers across the city. What started as a humble dream has become a beloved tradition built on good food, warm hospitality, and endless conversations.

THE SECRET BEHIND OUR CHAI

Great chai is an art, not a shortcut. Here is what makes our brew special:

100% Fresh Milk: We use pure, unadulterated milk to give our tea its signature rich, velvety texture.

Traditional Clay Pots (Matka): Simmering and serving in clay pots infuses an authentic, earthy aroma you cannot replicate in metal cups.

Hand-Ground Cardamom & Spices: We crush fresh cardamom, ginger, and spices by hand every morning to unlock maximum flavor.

FRESH INGREDIENTS & STUDENT-FRIENDLY PRICING

We believe high quality shouldn't come with a high price tag. Every batch of puris, parathas, and samosas is prepared fresh daily using whole ingredients. Located right near campus, we keep our prices pocket-friendly so every student can enjoy a satisfying meal and a steaming cup of tea without breaking the bank.
